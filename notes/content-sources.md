was not available, the text says so rather than filling the gap.

## What is illustrative rather than real

These are design behaviours, not factual claims, and each is labelled in the interface:

- Video blocks. Poster images are real photographs. Pressing play shows a panel explaining
  that the player would load at that point. There is no real film.
- Search overlay. Static UI with an example query already typed. Chips toggle, they do not filter.
- Forms. Enquiry, resource download, event registration and newsletter are UI only.
  Submitting shows a note saying the concept does not submit anything.
- "Download as PDF" on the article is visual only.
- Reading time on the article is calculated from the actual word count, 799 words, at
  about 200 words per minute.
- Launcher step card thumbnails are screenshots of these mockup pages, not Waterstons assets.

## Confidentiality

A few facts came from the client directly rather than from a public page. Those rows are
marked "Supplied by the client" above. Nothing in this repo quotes or paraphrases that
material, and none of it is reproduced here.

Two decisions worth recording:

- One figure available to us appears on no public Waterstons page, so it is used nowhere
  in the build.
- An earlier draft carried a positioning line that could not be traced to a public page.
  It was removed and replaced with copy from the published About Us page.
