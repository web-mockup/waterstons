Concept design · website redesign

Almost nobody starts at the homepage.

Real visitors arrive sideways. An article a colleague shared, a name that came up at an event, a search after a board meeting. So rather than three pages in isolation, here is the route one of them actually takes.

Follow it in order, or jump to any page. Every site page has a Show our thinking button that reveals why each section is there. This launcher is a guide to the three pages, not part of the proposed site.

01 The journey
  • 00 A post in someone's feedA CFO or IT Director sees an article shared by a colleague, recognises the problem, and taps the link. No homepage involved. →
  • 01 They land on the articleFirst contact with the brand is a piece of writing, not a pitch. Named author, real face, four minutes, and a clear way through to the service underneath it. →
  • 02 "Can these people actually help?"The Cyber page, doubling as the reusable service page template. Plain English problems, six services, accreditations, a case study with real numbers, five straight answers and a short form. →
  • 03 "So who are Waterstons?"Only now does the homepage matter. Scale, clients, sectors, the family business story, and the people. This is the due diligence stop, and it ends in a contact. →

Every photograph, client, accreditation, case study, statistic and event on these pages is real and published by Waterstons, each traced to the public page it came from in notes/content-sources.md. Nothing has been invented. Concept only, not a live site.
